Primary mean and variance are constant-location/scale Student-t maximum likelihood estimates, fixed df=5.
Variance = scale^2 * 5/3. Raw sample moments are retained in raw_* columns.
Baseline/post-test: scored valid finite components. Training: finite labeled values excluding outlier=1; no status/review exclusion.
Variance axes use symlog (linear below 0.01 strength squared / 0.1 deg squared); no points trimmed.
Each small point is one participant paired at baseline (x) and post-test (y).
For each figure, panel and training group, a full-scatter bivariate Student-t (df=5) is fitted to the complete participant pairs in original units.
Black-edged population points show the fitted location. Each pair has a joint weight of 7/(5 + squared Mahalanobis distance) during fitting.
Insets reuse the main-panel centers. The figures show point estimates; uncertainty in individual stage estimates is not propagated.
population_points.csv records locations, scatter matrix entries (model covariance = 5/3 times scatter), paired IDs and fit status.
Use --metrics-csv subject_baseline_vs_transfer_metrics.csv with the path to the saved participant table to redraw from its coordinates.
Companion statistics tables report the original arithmetic means and marginal SEMs.
Primary overview split into separate 180 x 170 mm mean/variance figures; companion three-rule PDF/CSV/Markdown statistics tables.
Paired tests in CSV are exploratory, unadjusted and do not propagate stage-estimation uncertainty.
Post-test combines trained-task retention and untrained-task transfer depending on group.
Censoring requires value at recorded cap plus uncapped estimate beyond cap; likelihood uses tail probability.
